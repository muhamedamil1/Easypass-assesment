# ExecPlans

Use one ExecPlan for this multi-stage assessment. It is a living repository document and must remain understandable without chat history.

Required sections:

## Goal

Concrete user/system outcome.

## Scope and non-goals

What is included and intentionally excluded.

## Source of truth

Exact repository contracts governing the work.

## Time budget

Planned and actual active time, with scope reduction when the limit is reached.

## Progress

- `[ ]` pending
- `[~]` in progress
- `[x]` complete
- `[!]` blocked

## Decisions and discoveries

Verified facts, deviations, agent mistakes, human decisions, and linked decision IDs.

## Milestones

Small ordered stages. Each states expected files, behaviors, and completion proof.

## Validation

Commands and scenarios that must pass.

## Results

Delivered behavior, evidence paths, real AI correction, known limitations, and production escalation.

Do not turn the plan into a speculative essay. Update it immediately after each bounded task and verification run.
